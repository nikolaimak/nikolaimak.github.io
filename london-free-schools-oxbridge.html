ℹ️ About the data

School types
  • Grammar — fully selective state schools. Entry by 11+ exam only. Free. London has 19, all shown (per Education Act 1998 Statutory Instrument).
  • State (Top 80) — comprehensive schools that made the Oxbridge Top 80 list. Formally non-selective, but often use faith criteria, banding, or interviews that effectively select.
  • Partially selective — state schools that select 10–33% of intake by ability or aptitude (music, art, languages, sport, general ability). The rest admitted by distance/faith. Free. Source: Wikipedia's statutory list + known aptitude selectors.
  • Independent — private fee-paying schools. Colour intensity = fee level (£28k–£64k/year).
Oxbridge Top 80

Schools ranked by number of offers from Oxford + Cambridge (most recent published data). Larger dots = in the Top 80. Rank, offer count, and percentage shown in popup.

Completeness
  • Grammar: Complete — all 19 London grammar schools per statutory list.
  • Partially selective: All schools from the Wikipedia statutory list (pre-1997 arrangements preserved by law) + well-known aptitude selectors. Not every academy with a 10% aptitude band is listed — there are dozens more.
  • State (non-selective): Only those that made the Oxbridge Top 80.
  • Independent: Only those in the Oxbridge Top 80. Many more independent schools exist in London.
Entry points

11+ = Year 7 entry (age 10–11). 13+ = Year 9 entry. 16+ = Sixth Form entry. Some single-sex schools open to both genders at 16+.

Sources: Education (Grammar School Designation) Order 1998; Wikipedia: List of grammar schools in England & Partially selective school (England); Oxford/Cambridge admissions data via league tables.
